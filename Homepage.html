What is Dev Blox?

One place for everything Roblox development

Dev Blox brings together in-depth guides, a community of builders and scripters, and the latest platform news — so you don't have to piece together answers from a dozen different forums and outdated tutorials. Whether you're scripting your first script or shipping your fifth game, everything you need lives here.

Why Developers Choose Dev Blox

Built to save you time and get you unstuck

  • Guides written by developers, not generic tutorial mills — real answers to real scripting and building problems.
  • A community that actually replies — post what you're stuck on and get feedback from people who've shipped before you.
  • Studio and platform updates summarized, so you know what changed before it breaks your project.
Our Story

Built by developers who got tired of searching

Dev Blox started as a shared frustration: good Roblox scripting knowledge was scattered across old forum posts, outdated videos, and Discord servers that had already moved on. We wanted one place that stayed current, gave straight answers, and treated the community as the actual point — not an afterthought. That's still what we're building toward today.
