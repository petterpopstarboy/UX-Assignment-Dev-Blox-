Basic Controls

Understanding the basic controls in Roblox Studio is essential for building and navigating your projects. These controls allow you to move the camera, select objects, and manipulate parts within the 3D workspace.

Before you continue, adjust your graphics level if you have a lower performing device. (File > Studio Settings > Editor Graphics Level)

Camera Controls

  • Right Mouse Button: Hold and drag to rotate the camera view.
  • W, A, S, D Keys: Move the camera forward, left, backward, and right.
  • Q / E Keys: Move the camera down (Q) or up (E).
  • Shift: Hold to move the camera faster.
  • Mouse Scroll Wheel: Zoom the camera in and out.

Object Controls

  • Select Tool (1 key): Click to select parts or models in the workspace.
  • Move Tool (2 key): Allows you to reposition parts along the X, Y, and Z axes.
  • Scale Tool (3 key): Resize parts by dragging handles.
  • Rotate Tool (4 key): Rotate parts around different axes.
  • Transfrom Tool (5 key): The hybrid of Move, Scale and Rotate with an addditional feature to select a plane for movement.

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Ctrl + Z: Undo the last action.
  • Ctrl + Y: Redo an undone action.
  • F: Focus the camera on the selected object.
  • Ctrl + D: Duplicate the selected object.
  • Delete/Backspace: Remove the selected object from the workspace.

View

They can be found in the 'view' tab of studio.

  • Explorer: Contains all your object locations.
  • Properties: Contains special properties of a currently selected object.
  • Toolbox: Contains all free models that are publicly available. Beware for potential scripts in them as some could contain viruses!
  • Output: Displays all prints and errors from scripts.

Best Practices

  • Use the F key often to quickly center the camera on your work.
  • Keep one hand on the mouse and the other on WASD keys for efficient navigation.
  • Learn keyboard shortcuts to speed up your workflow.
  • Anchor parts when building static structures to prevent unwanted movement.
Note: You can change your keybinds for a more preferred set-up to enhance your effeciency.
